Internship at Toyota Saga Toyopet

Project Intern · Saga, Japan · 2 weeks, Oct 2026

Completed as part of MIRAI-Setu, with Dr. Manish Kumar Thakur of IIITDM Jabalpur as program leader. The certification letter, signed by the CEO of Saga Toyopet, lists two assignments, and I worked on both.

The work I did

Assignment 1

Tuk-Tuk reservation system, Kashima

An interactive prototype of a smart tuk-tuk rental service for tourists in Kashima, Saga. It connects the tourist app, the Tourist Information Center portal, and the Saga Toyopet portal.

  • Seven-step journey: enter, book, verify, pay, unlock, explore, return.
  • Dynamic pricing for weekends, peak hours, and demand.
  • English, Japanese, Chinese, and Korean.

Assignment 2

Document digitalization, Smile Passport

A prototype that replaces the paper forms for Saga Toyopet's courtesy (loaner) car service with a guided online request, in Japanese and English.

  • Customer enters details, agrees to the terms, and signs on screen.
  • Customer tracks the request through approval, loan, and return.
  • Staff view to approve, assign a vehicle, and record handover and return.
